Machine Learning · Customer Analytics

Insurance Customer Segmentation & Churn Prediction

A combined unsupervised and supervised learning workflow for understanding customer behavior and identifying higher-risk churn profiles.

Overview

Project overview

Insurance businesses need both descriptive customer segments and predictive risk signals. This project combines clustering and classification to create a more complete customer analytics workflow.

Architecture

Customer Data→EDA→Cleaning→Feature Engineering→PCA→K-Means→Random Forest / XGBoost→Evaluation

Customer Segmentation

PCA reduces dimensionality and helps summarize high-dimensional customer behavior. K-Means groups customers into interpretable segments for exploratory analysis.

Churn Prediction

Random Forest and XGBoost models are used to estimate churn risk based on engineered customer features and behavioral patterns.

Business Interpretation

The workflow connects model outputs back to customer segments so analysis can support retention strategies and targeted business decisions.

Technology Stack

PythonPandasNumPyScikit-learnK-MeansPCARandom ForestXGBoost

Key Learning

Combining clustering with predictive modeling provides both an explanation of customer structure and a forward-looking estimate of churn risk.
